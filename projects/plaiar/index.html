CASAGRANDE

A WebAR gaming platform designed to turn live audiences into active participants through short mobile experiences during sports events.

Co-founder & Product Designer • 2024 • Sports & Entertainment

Co-founder & Product Designer • 2024 • Sports & Entertainment

From Funding to a Real Pilot

ANII and ANDE backed both the MVP and its dissemination. That funded a real test — not a demo, but a live basketball game at Club Welcome, with a crowd that had other things to pay attention to.

Building the Core Experience

The MVP ran directly in the phone browser — no app, no installation. A QR code at the venue was the only entry point. The camera turned the court into the game environment, and the challenge had to be understood before the next play started: a fan in the middle of a game won't stop to read instructions.

Turning a Game into a Product

One event is a demo. We built a configuration layer around the core game — branding, scoring, rankings, rewards — so the same mechanics could run for any club or sponsor without rebuilding.

Creating Value for Sponsors

Fans who played could unlock branded rewards from sponsors. That gave sponsors an active interaction during the event — not just a logo on a banner.

Testing with a Real Audience

The launch at Club Welcome revealed what lab testing couldn't. Fans in the stands have limited attention and no patience for a slow entry. Device variety was real. Venue connectivity was inconsistent. Every friction point that didn't matter in a prototype mattered in front of a crowd with somewhere else to look.

Reducing the Barrier to Play

Entry had to feel like part of the event — not a separate decision. QR codes placed around the venue opened the experience directly in the browser: no download, no account, no steps between scanning and playing.

What the Pilot Showed

Running the activation made the commercial model clear. A custom experience for one club is a service. A configurable platform that any club or sponsor can deploy across events is a product. plaiAR moved in that direction.
